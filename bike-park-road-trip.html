03 Before You Go

  • Loam Pass covers Highland, Sugarloaf, Empire 47, Vallée Bras-du-Nord, Le Massif, Burke, and Kingdom Trails (2 visits each).
  • Sugarloaf lift runs Fri–Sun only — Sunday works as planned; confirm the weekend chair hours before counting on it.
  • Wednesday is the transition day — ride Empire 47 in the morning, then a ~4 hr evening drive to East Burke. Fuel up and eat before you go; you'll roll in late.
  • Passports — bring them for everyone. Required at both border crossings: into Canada Sunday at Coburn Gore, and back into the US Wednesday on the QC→VT leg.
  • Two border crossings — into Canada at Coburn Gore (Sun) and back into the US on the QC→VT leg (Wed). Be set to declare the bikes.
  • Quebec City is home base Sun–Tue nights — three rides (Empire 47, Le Massif, Vallée Bras-du-Nord) all within a short drive, with dining in Old Town Quebec City nearby.
