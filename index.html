Features

Built for serious training

Smart Trainer Control

Controls any Bluetooth FTMS trainer, plus Wahoo KICKR and Tacx FE-C natively. ERG mode holds the target for structured workouts; simulation mode sets grade and rolling resistance for routes and free rides, surface by surface; resistance mode leaves you on your own gears for tests and intervals. Trainers reconnect on their own after a dropout, and sensor battery levels show before you start.

Route Simulation

Ride real-world routes from Ride with GPS or GPX files. Your trainer responds to every climb and descent with realistic grade resistance, and to the road itself: a Ride with GPS route carries its surface, so gravel and dirt roll harder than pavement. A satellite 3D map rides beside the elevation profile.

Ride with GPS

Connect your Ride with GPS account and your route library is in the app on every device. Import a route to ride it indoors with its real surface, or follow it outdoors. Upload finished rides, and turn an enduro ride into a route with its stages marked as points of interest.

Outdoor GPS Rides

One tap to ride on iPhone. Follow a route with a 3D navigation camera and the course drawn on the map, take a structured workout outside with audio coaching, or just pedal. Rides are saved as you go, tagged road, mountain, or e-bike at the end, and uploaded with the right bike.

Enduro Mode

Alternating liaison and stage laps with a countdown into each stage, on iPhone and Apple Watch. Follow a stage-marked course and the stages open and close themselves at the gates. Per-stage stats, route and elevation colored by stage, uploads to Strava as a mountain bike ride, and a finished ride becomes a Ride with GPS route with each stage marked as a point of interest.

Adaptive Training Intelligence

A daily suggestion built from your rides — your form, the days you actually ride and rest, your discipline, and your race calendar. The season runs in blocks around your A races, with deload weeks, openers, and a progression block that raises one capability at a time. Each day's workout is one tap to start, indoors or out, with coaching that explains it in plain language and a 4-week outlook of what's ahead.

Power Profile

Your power-duration curve builds from the rides you already do — no test required. See critical power, your rider phenotype, and the duration band your discipline demands. Capability tracking follows your punch, climbing, durability, and efficiency; Training Focus names the band your goal needs; and short, hard efforts in workouts are targeted from your own curve, never a fixed percentage of FTP.

Race Calendar

Add races with a priority, discipline, goals, and registration status, and the season plans itself around them: A races set the build and taper, every race gets an easy day before and recovery after, and race-day coaching tells you what to do. Attach a course to preview it, ride it on the trainer, or follow it on race day. Results link to your ride, and races export to your iCloud calendar.

Training Trips

Multi-day trips, camps, and destination riding get their own place on the calendar, with a plan for each day: venue, discipline, how hard, and which days are off or travel. Your plan tapers into the trip and recovers out of it, the coaching switches to companion mode while you're there, and a trip marked as a primary goal has the build ramp toward it. Trips sync everywhere and export to your iCloud calendar.

FTP Testing

Test when you want to, not because you have to. Three built-in protocols — a ramp test in ERG mode, and 20-minute and 2×8-minute tests ridden on your own gears, indoors or outdoors — detect your FTP at the finish and offer to update it, with every result kept in your test history. Between tests, your power-duration curve keeps a running estimate from the rides you already do.

Ride Analysis

Per-second charts for power, heart rate, cadence, and speed, a map, lap and stage breakdowns, and time in zone. Power distribution shows where your watts lived; peak power marks your best at every duration against your 90-day best. No power meter? Load is estimated from heart rate so the ride still counts. Descent load and late-ride fade surface for gravity rides, and the coach notes what the ride did for your week.

Apple Watch

Ride standalone with GPS, power, and cadence paired through watchOS. Free rides, structured workouts, enduro with automatic stage gates, mountain bike and e-bike rides, from the Action Button or Siri. Five ride pages on the Digital Crown, including a map you can switch off to save battery. Every ride is saved as you go and secured the moment you tap End, then lands on your iPhone, in iCloud, and in Apple Health.

Wet Mode

Rain on a touchscreen registers as taps — enough of it can end a ride by itself. Arm Wet Mode before a wet ride and the Watch screen goes fully dead using native Water Lock. The Action Button still marks laps and stages without unlocking; turn the Digital Crown to unlock for a look, and the screen locks itself again. It stays armed for the whole ride and releases cleanly when you end it.

Apple TV

Full rides on the big screen with the Siri Remote. Structured workouts, Ride with GPS and GPX routes on a satellite map, and free rides with trainer control; the day's suggestion from the coach; ride history with charts and elevation; athlete switching. Connect Strava, Intervals.icu, and Ride with GPS by scanning a QR code with your phone. Rides sync everywhere and reach Apple Health through your iPhone.

Multi-Athlete Support

Individual profiles with separate FTP, power zones, ride history, coaching, race calendar, surfaces, default bikes, and platform connections for each athlete. Switch profiles instantly, on every device.

iCloud Sync

Ride history, workouts, race calendar, trips, athlete profiles, and the coach's own state sync across your Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV via iCloud, in your private container. Plan on one device, ride on another, rename a ride anywhere.

Workout Library

Stock templates for endurance, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, and more, plus discipline-specific sessions the coach draws on for the day's suggestion. Create custom workouts with the built-in editor or import ZWO and MRC files, ride any of them indoors or outdoors with audio coaching, and send them to your Watch for standalone structured rides.

Workout Generator

Need a session now? Pick a type — endurance, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, sprint, neuromuscular, recovery, or enduro — set the duration and interval length, and ride it. The enduro generator builds a race simulation from your stage count, liaison length, and stage mix, short and punchy to long and technical. On Mac, iPhone, and Apple TV, with every generated workout saved to your library.
